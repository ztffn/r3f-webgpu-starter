Distant Front

A true spiritual successor to Delta Force, built by modders and mapmakers from the original NovaLogic community. Vast battlefields, long-range lethality, concealment that matters, and missions where patience beats spectacle.

The mission

Bring back the battlefield.

Distant Front is not a modern hero shooter wearing an old name. It is a deliberate continuation of the design language that made the early Delta Force games distinctive: enormous terrain, vulnerable soldiers, uncertain sightlines, sparse information and the constant tension between movement and exposure.

The technology is new. The priorities are not.

Design pillars

Fieldcraft over fireworks.

01

Distance is gameplay

Terrain, optics, ballistics and visibility shape every encounter.

02

Grass is concealment

03

Small teams, large spaces

Recon and ranger-scale operations across battlefields that feel genuinely open.

04

Legacy without imitation

Original technology, art and missions carrying forward a recognizable design philosophy.

Community

Return to the long grass.

Development is early and community-led. The first milestone is a playable combat field: terrain, grass concealment, long-range weapon handling, AI and a small objective-driven mission.
