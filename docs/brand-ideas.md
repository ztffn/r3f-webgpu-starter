The name should feel like it could have existed alongside Delta Force in 1999.
An original long-range tactical FPS inspired by late-1990s and early-2000s PC military shooters.

ranger / recon / long-range insertion is probably the richest lane.

The brand should evoke distance, observation, concealment, terrain, and patience, rather than just elite operators.
Initial community strong with old school Delta Force players, no real spiritual succesor until this project, but recent DF revival is popular with new players in a much more spectacle way.
